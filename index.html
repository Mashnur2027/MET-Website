Mission

Our mission is to translate biomechanical science into practical, interpretable indicators that can help ergonomics and safety stakeholders improve decision-making and reduce musculoskeletal disorder (MSD) risk—especially low back injury risk—in modern work settings.

Technology under development

Biomechanics foundation

The approach is grounded in spine biomechanics and neuromuscular control, including muscle coactivation dynamics during complex tasks.

Analytics engine

A developing analytics workflow designed to generate ergonomic risk indicators from task factors and biomechanical features.

Practical application

The goal is to make advanced metrics easier to apply—supporting applied ergonomics assessments, intervention evaluation, and program planning.

Status: Conceptual to early applied development. Planned future deployment includes SaaS-based analytics, with optional on-site implementation, training, and expert interpretation as applied layers.

Applied use cases (examples)

Industrial ergonomics & MSD prevention

Manual material handling, repetitive tasks, non-neutral trunk postures, and fatigue-sensitive work in industrial and logistics environments.

High-demand work environments

Understanding how pacing pressure, monitoring or surveillance, and load uncertainty may influence movement strategies and spinal loading.
